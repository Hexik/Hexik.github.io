# Embryo Gomoku – Privacy Policy

[Česky](privacy-cs)

Last updated: 2026-10-23

## Who is responsible

Ing. Miroslav Fontán, 561 Písecká, Blatná, 388 01, Czech Republic
E-mail: embryo.gomoku@gmail.com

This policy covers the Android app "Embryo Gomoku" (the "app").

## Summary

- The game engine runs entirely on your device. Your games are never sent to us.
- The app has no user accounts and does not ask for your name, e-mail or contacts.
- Advertising (Google AdMob) and crash reporting (Firebase Crashlytics) are provided by
  Google. Where the law requires it, they run only after you agree in the consent dialog.
- We do not sell personal data.

## Data stored only on your device

| Data | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Settings (level, rule, board, theme, language) | Remember your preferences |
| The game in progress and games you save | Continue or reload a game |
| Archive of played games (up to 500, of which up to 100 pinned) | Let you review earlier games |
| Statistics (wins, losses, draws per rule and level) and the rating estimate | Show your results |

This data never leaves the device through the app. It is included in the standard
Android backup, so if you have backup enabled, Android may copy it to your Google
account; that backup is governed by Google's terms and your device settings. You can
delete the data at any time: reset statistics or delete archived games in the app,
clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app.

When you choose **Share game**, the app passes the game record (SGF text) to the app
you pick. What happens next is up to that app.

## Data processed by third parties

### Advertising – Google AdMob

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect the advertising ID,
IP address, device and app information and ad interactions, in order to show, measure
and limit ads and to prevent fraud. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the app asks for
your consent first (Google User Messaging Platform); without consent only
non-personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the app's
privacy settings, and you can reset or delete the advertising ID in Android
settings.
Google privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
How Google uses data from partner apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

### Crash reporting – Firebase Crashlytics

If the app or the game engine crashes, Crashlytics sends a crash report: the stack
trace, device model, Android version, app version, a random installation identifier
and, for an engine failure, the game rule, the last engine commands and the current
board position. It contains no name, e-mail or advertising ID. Reports are sent only
after you have agreed in the consent dialog. They are used only to fix bugs and are
kept for 90 days.
Firebase privacy information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

### Purchases – Google Play Billing

If you buy the Profi version, the payment is handled entirely by Google Play. The app
only receives a confirmation that the purchase exists; we never see your payment
details.

## Legal bases (GDPR)

- Personalised ads and crash reports: your **consent** (Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can
  withdraw at any time.
- Non-personalised ads and fraud prevention: our **legitimate interest** in funding the
  free app (Art. 6(1)(f)).
- Purchases: **performance of a contract** (Art. 6(1)(b)).

Data processed by Google may be transferred outside the EU; Google relies on the EU–US
Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses.

## Children

The app is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children.

## Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase or restrict the processing of your
personal data, to object to processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent.
Because we hold no data that identifies you, most requests are best handled directly in
the app or through Google (for ads and crash data). You can contact us at the e-mail
above, and you may lodge a complaint with the Czech data protection authority
(Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů, https://uoou.gov.cz).

## Changes

We will update this page when the app changes how it handles data. The date at the top
shows the latest version.
